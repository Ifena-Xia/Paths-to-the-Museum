# Paths to the Museum

**Live site:** https://ifena-xia.github.io/Paths-to-the-Museum/

A quantitative study examining how parental education and geographic location shape children's cultural participation in China. Using data from the China Education Panel Survey (CEPS, 2013–2014 cohort), the paper models museum and cultural institution visit frequency among junior high school students across urban, semi-urban, and rural areas.

## Methods

OLS regression, binary logistic regression, and multinomial logistic regression, with interaction terms between parental education and urbanicity level.

## Data

China Education Panel Survey (CEPS), wave 1 (2013–2014). Analytic sample: 9,761 families with complete and internally consistent reports from both student and parent questionnaires.

## Key finding

Parental education and urban residence are the strongest predictors of children's museum participation. Higher parental education partially offsets the rural disadvantage, and its positive effect is stronger in lower-urbanicity settings.
